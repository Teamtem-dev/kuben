A project can publish a **status page**. Anyone can open it without signing in, at `https://<console address>/status/<slug>`.

## Publish a page

Open the project and find the **Public status page** card. Set the **Address** (the slug), the **Title** and the **Environments shown** (production is ticked to begin with), and press **Publish**. **Open page** opens it; **Take down** removes it. Publishing needs the `admin` role or higher.

Or with the API:

```bash
curl -X PUT -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"slug":"shop","title":"Shop","environments":["production"]}' \
  https://kuben.example.com/api/v1/projects/shop/status-page
```

- **`slug`** must be unique across the installation: 3 to 63 lowercase letters, digits and dashes.
- **`title`** is 1 to 100 characters.
- **`environments`** lists whose apps the page shows (1 to 20).
- **`"enabled": false`** hides the page but keeps its settings.
- **`DELETE`** on the same address takes the page down at once.

## What the page shows

- **The overall state:** operational, degraded or major outage. It is the state of the worst service.
- **Each app** of the listed environments, by its display name, with its state:
  - **operational:** the app reports ready and all its pods are ready;
  - **degraded:** some pods are not ready, a critical or warning incident is open, or Kuben has no report of the app yet;
  - **major outage:** the app has pods and none of them is ready, or it reports not ready and has no pods.

  If Kuben has not observed an app, the page never calls it operational.
- **Incidents** of those apps: the open ones and those resolved in the last 7 days (the newest 50), with their severity and times.

It never shows:

- namespaces, pods, nodes, addresses or images;
- incident titles, details or error codes;
- apps of other environments, and incidents that concern the whole installation.

The same data is available as JSON at `GET /api/v1/public/status/<slug>`. Answers are cached for 15 seconds (`Cache-Control: public, max-age=15`), and the console page refreshes every 30 seconds. The page follows the visitor's language (English or Persian) and light or dark theme.
