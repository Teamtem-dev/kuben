This policy covers the website **kuben.teamtem.com**, operated by Teamtem. It does not cover a Kuben installation you run yourself: Kuben sends no data to Teamtem.

## What this website collects

**Nothing, while you read it.** The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. Your browser stores your light or dark theme choice locally; it never leaves your device. Like every website, requests pass through our hosting provider, Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site.

**What you send through the enterprise form.** When you submit the form on the [Enterprise](/enterprise/) page we receive:

- your name, company, email address, the topic you chose and your message;
- the country your request came from and your browser's user-agent string, both reported by Cloudflare.

We do **not** store your IP address. To limit abuse, a one-way hash of it is kept for at most one hour to count how many messages come from the same network.

## Why

Only to answer your inquiry and to follow up on it. We do not sell or rent this information, do not add you to a mailing list, and do not use it for advertising.

## Where and for how long

Form submissions are stored in Cloudflare Workers KV, a service of our hosting provider, and are **deleted automatically after 180 days**. If a notification channel is configured, a short summary of the message is also posted to a private channel of the Kuben team.

## Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete what you sent through the form. Contact Teamtem through [teamtem.com](https://teamtem.com) or send a new message through the form, and mention the email address you used.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
