# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Argue about the code, not the person writing it.

## What we expect

- **Assume competence.** Someone reporting a bug badly, or proposing a design
  you disagree with, is trying to make the project better. Ask what they meant
  before telling them they are wrong.
- **Critique the work.** "This drops errors on the reconcile path" is useful.
  "This is amateur work" is not, and says nothing about the code.
- **Accept a no.** Maintainers will decline features that do not fit Kuben's
  invariants — one small binary, Kubernetes as the source of truth, secure by
  default. A declined idea is not a personal rejection.
- **Respect effort.** Reviews take time and so do contributions. Say what needs
  changing clearly enough that the other person can act on it.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment in any form; insults or personal attacks; discriminatory remarks
about who someone is; sexualized language or imagery; publishing someone's
private information; sustained disruption of discussions; encouraging any of
the above.

This applies everywhere the project happens: issues, pull requests, commit
messages, code comments and any venue where you are representing Kuben.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers at **mwmixdev@gmail.com**. Include links
and enough context to understand what happened. Reports are read by the
maintainers only, and we will not share the reporter's identity with the person
being reported without asking first.

If a report concerns a maintainer, say so — it will be handled by the others.

## What happens next

Maintainers will look into it and respond. Depending on what we find, the
outcome ranges from a private word, to editing or removing content, to blocking
an account from the repository. Serious or repeated behaviour skips the earlier
steps.

We aim to acknowledge a report within a few days. This is a small project, not
a company: the process is people reading your message and acting on it, and we
would rather be honest about that than promise a service level we cannot meet.
